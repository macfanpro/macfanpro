This release tidies two parts of the interface. Based on ThermalForge 0.2.3.

## What's changed

- **Background service row:** the entry for setting up or removing the background service is now a row in the menu, below Updates. Its **Manage…** button matches **Check for Updates**, replacing the small link next to Quit. In Chinese the label is 服务 / 服務, two characters like the rows above it. All 18 languages are updated.
- **Setup window:** the window no longer has its own language picker. It follows the language chosen in the menu.

Fan control, curves and protection thresholds are unchanged.

本版整理了界面上的两处细节。

### 更新内容

- **后台服务一行**：安装或移除后台服务的入口改为菜单中“更新”下方的一行，标签为“服务”，与“语言”“版本”“更新”一样是两个字；右侧“管理…”按钮与“检查更新”样式一致。原来“退出”旁边的小字链接已移除。18 种语言均已更新。

风扇控制、温控曲线和保护阈值均未改变。
