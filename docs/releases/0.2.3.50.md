MacFanPro now includes a DMG with graphical background-service setup, an 18-language website, and upstream connection and installer hardening. Based on ThermalForge 0.2.3.

## What's changed

- **Drag-and-drop installation**: download the DMG, drag MacFanPro into Applications, and open it. The setup window asks for macOS administrator authorization to install the bundled background service. No Terminal, Homebrew, Xcode or further download is needed for this step.
- **Service setup in 18 languages**: synchronize the app and service, retry cancelled or failed authorization, and remove the service while keeping settings and logs. Existing Homebrew installations keep their update channel; another account's service and explicit CLI fan holds are protected.
- **Connection and installer hardening**: merge upstream peer-identity checks, connection/log limits and safe app-attribute cleanup, while retaining MacFanPro's disconnect protection and hardware-operation timeout handling.
- **Website and documentation**: the [project website](https://macfanpro.github.io/macfanpro/en/) supports 18 languages. The English and Chinese README now combine installation guidance with fan-curve, safety and developer reference material.

Homebrew, the online installer and source builds remain available. Thermal curves and the 95/90°C protection thresholds are unchanged. Version numbering advances from 0.2.3.37 to 0.2.3.50; withdrawn releases are not restored.

## Upgrading

- **Homebrew**: use the existing Homebrew update flow, then synchronize the app and service as instructed. The tap and prebuilt bottle update after publication.
- **Online installer**: use **Update in Terminal** or run the online command again.
- **DMG**: quit the app, replace it in Applications, and reopen it. Authorize service synchronization if prompted. To remove a DMG installation, choose **Background service… → Remove background service…** before moving the app to the Trash.

## Verification and limits

- [Release CI](https://github.com/macfanpro/macfanpro/actions/runs/37226140808) passed Debug/Release, disconnect, installer and packaging checks. All six draft assets were downloaded; checksums, strict app signatures, versions, 18 language catalogs and DMG/tar app equality were verified.
- The [version-specific validation record](https://github.com/macfanpro/macfanpro/blob/main/docs/macfanpro-0.2.3.50-validation.md) records completed source, CI, downloaded-package and installation-channel checks separately.
- **Real administrator authorization, first installation on a clean Mac, service upgrade/removal and thermal/sleep-wake hardware acceptance were not performed for this release.** Simulated setup tests and package verification do not establish these results.
- Packages retain **ad-hoc signing and are not notarized by Apple**. Follow the [first-launch instructions](https://github.com/macfanpro/macfanpro#macos-says-it-cant-verify-the-developer) if macOS blocks opening.
