This release stops the background service from writing a log line for every step of a fan ramp. Based on ThermalForge 0.2.3.

## What's changed

### Fixed

- **Excessive log writes**: Smart mode changes fan speed in small steps, about ten times a second, and the background service logged every step. On an M4 Max that was about 100,000 lines in five hours. The daily log filled within hours, and macOS recorded disk-write reports for `macfanpro`.
  - Each fan now logs at most one line every 5 seconds, noting how many ramp steps were skipped since the previous line.
  - Setting the fans to max and resetting them to Apple defaults are still logged immediately.
  - Fan control itself is unchanged, and so are curves and protection thresholds.

## 中文说明

本次更新让后台服务不再为风扇平滑调速的每一步都写一行日志。基于 ThermalForge 0.2.3。

### 更新内容

- **修复 日志写入过多**：智能模式以每秒约 10 次的小步调整风扇转速，后台服务把每一步都写进日志。在 M4 Max 上，5 小时约 10 万行，当天日志几小时就写满，macOS 也为 `macfanpro` 记录了磁盘写入超限报告。
  - 现在每个风扇最多每 5 秒写一行，并注明距上一行省略了多少步；
  - 风扇设为最大转速、恢复 Apple 默认控制仍会立即记录；
  - 风扇控制本身、温控曲线和保护阈值均未改变。

### 下载与安装

要求 **Apple Silicon Mac、macOS 14 或更高版本**。下载文件见上方表格。

选择一种安装方式：[在线安装脚本](https://github.com/macfanpro/macfanpro/blob/main/README.zh-CN.md#在线安装脚本) · [Homebrew](https://github.com/macfanpro/macfanpro/blob/main/README.zh-CN.md#方式一通过-homebrew-安装)（预编译，无需 Xcode） · [下载发行包](https://github.com/macfanpro/macfanpro/blob/main/README.zh-CN.md#方式二下载发行包安装) · [源码构建](https://github.com/macfanpro/macfanpro/blob/main/README.zh-CN.md#方式三从源码构建安装)。Homebrew 7 需要先执行一次 `brew trust macfanpro/tap`。
