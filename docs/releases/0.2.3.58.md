This release adds a settings window and makes the menu bar panel shorter. Based on ThermalForge 0.2.3.

## What's changed

- **Settings window:** choose **Settings…** at the bottom of the panel, or press ⌘, while the panel is open. A native window groups the preferences:
  - **General:** language, temperature unit (°C/°F) and Launch at Login.
  - **Updates:** the version and **Check for Updates**.
  - **Background service:** whether the service is running, with setup and removal. First-run and service synchronization use this window too.
- **A shorter panel:** the panel keeps the temperatures, fan speeds and profiles. When a release is available, it shows one "{version} available" row that opens the update details, and a dot appears on **Settings…**.
- All 18 languages are updated, including the right-to-left Arabic layout.

Fan control, curves and protection thresholds are unchanged.

## 中文说明

本版新增设置窗口，菜单栏面板更简洁。

### 更新内容

- **设置窗口**：点面板底部的“设置…”，或在面板打开时按 ⌘,，打开系统风格的设置窗口：
  - **通用**：语言、温度单位（°C/°F）、登录时启动；
  - **更新**：版本号与“检查更新”；
  - **服务**：后台服务是否在运行，以及安装与移除。首次安装和同步后台服务也在这个窗口完成。
- **更简洁的面板**：面板只保留温度、转速和模式。有新版本时显示一行“有新版本 …”，点击打开更新详情；“设置…”前也会出现蓝点。
- 18 种语言均已更新，包括从右到左排版的阿拉伯语。

风扇控制、温控曲线和保护阈值均未改变。
