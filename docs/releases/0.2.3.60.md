This release aligns the icons in the panel's **Quit** and **Settings** buttons, and opens the settings window centered on the screen. Based on ThermalForge 0.2.3.

## What's changed

- The icons in **Quit** and **Settings** are one size smaller, match the height of the text, and sit centered on it with 4 points of spacing. The tall exit icon no longer looks larger than its label.
- The settings window opens centered on the screen. It used to be centered before it grew to fit its content, so it appeared off to one side. Within a session, it reopens wherever you last moved it.

Fan control, curves and protection thresholds are unchanged.
