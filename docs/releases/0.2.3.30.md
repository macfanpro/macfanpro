This release adds Arabic, the 18th interface language, with a mirrored right-to-left layout. Based on ThermalForge 0.2.3.

## What's changed

### New

- **Arabic**: the menu bar app is now available in Arabic. For right-to-left text the whole panel is mirrored — labels on the right, values and buttons on the left — while commands, RPM and temperatures stay left to right. Follow System picks Arabic when it is first in your system's language order. Translations were made by the maintainer; corrections from native speakers are welcome.

Fan control, curves and protection thresholds are unchanged.

## Upgrading

Quit the menu bar app before updating, then install the new version and reopen it. After a Homebrew update, sync the background service and the app too. See [Updating](https://github.com/macfanpro/macfanpro#updating).

## 中文说明

本次更新新增第 18 种界面语言阿拉伯语，并支持从右往左的镜像排版。基于 ThermalForge 0.2.3。

### 更新内容

- **阿拉伯语**：菜单栏应用新增阿拉伯语界面。对于从右往左书写的文字，整个面板左右镜像——标签在右、数值和按钮在左，命令、RPM 和温度仍从左往右显示。系统语言顺序中阿拉伯语排在最前时，“跟随系统”会自动选用。翻译由维护者完成，欢迎母语用户指正。

风扇控制、温控曲线和保护阈值未改变。

### 下载与安装

要求 **Apple Silicon Mac、macOS 14 或更高版本**。下载文件见上方表格。

选择一种安装方式：[Homebrew](https://github.com/macfanpro/macfanpro/blob/main/README.zh-CN.md#方式一通过-homebrew-安装)（预编译，无需 Xcode） · [下载发行包](https://github.com/macfanpro/macfanpro/blob/main/README.zh-CN.md#方式二下载发行包安装) · [源码构建](https://github.com/macfanpro/macfanpro/blob/main/README.zh-CN.md#方式三从源码构建安装)。Homebrew 7 需要先执行一次 `brew trust macfanpro/tap`。

### 升级说明

更新前先退出菜单栏应用，再安装新版本并重新打开。Homebrew 更新后仍需同步后台服务和应用，详见 [更新步骤](https://github.com/macfanpro/macfanpro/blob/main/README.zh-CN.md#更新)。
