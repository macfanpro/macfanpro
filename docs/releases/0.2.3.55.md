This release adopts ThermalForge's fan release when the background service starts and stops, so fans can't be left under manual control after the service crashes or is restarted. Based on ThermalForge 0.2.3.

## What's changed

- **Fans released when the service starts:** if a previous service crashed or was force-killed while controlling the fans, or something wrote the fans directly, the starting service returns them to Apple's automatic control. If the reset fails, it retries.
- **Fans released when the service stops:** a stopped service (SIGTERM, as during installation or uninstallation) releases the fans it was controlling. Fans it does not control are left untouched.
- **Installation:** fans are returned to Apple's control before the service restarts, also when upgrading from earlier versions.
- **Unchanged on purpose:** if the app stops responding while the thermal floor holds the fans at maximum, MacFanPro keeps them at maximum until the Mac cools down. The Smart curve, 95°C / 90°C protection thresholds, logging and calibration are unchanged.

The start and stop logic is ThermalForge's own (#31), kept close to upstream to make future merges easy.

## 中文说明

本版采用 ThermalForge 上游在后台服务启动和停止时释放风扇的处理，避免服务崩溃或重启后风扇一直停在手动控制。

### 更新内容

- **服务启动时释放风扇**：之前的服务在控制风扇期间崩溃或被强制结束，或有程序直接写入了风扇，新启动的服务会把风扇交回 Apple 自动控制；交回失败会自动重试。
- **服务停止时释放风扇**：服务被停止时（SIGTERM，例如安装或卸载期间），会释放它正在控制的风扇；不是它控制的风扇不会动。
- **安装**：重启服务前先把风扇交回系统，从旧版本升级时也一样。
- **有意保留不变**：高温保护已把风扇拉到最大时，如果应用失去响应，MacFanPro 仍保持最大转速直到降温。Smart 曲线、95°C / 90°C 保护阈值、日志和校准都没有改变。

启动和停止的处理沿用 ThermalForge 上游代码（#31），尽量与上游一致，方便以后继续合并。

### 下载与升级

要求 **配备实体风扇的 Apple Silicon Mac、macOS 14 或更高版本**，下载见上表。

Homebrew 用户继续使用 Homebrew；DMG 用户退出应用，将新版拖入“应用程序”替换后重新打开，按提示同步后台服务，升级无需先卸载服务。在线脚本、代理及源码安装继续保留，见[安装教程](https://github.com/macfanpro/macfanpro/blob/main/README.zh-CN.md#安装)。
